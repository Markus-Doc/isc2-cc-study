# ISC2 CC Practice Quiz

---

## Question 1

**Triffid Corporation requires employees working with sensitive hardcopy documents to place them in a safe at the end of each workday.**

What type of control is the **process of putting the documents into the safe**?

A. Administrative  
B. Tangential  
C. Physical  
D. Technical  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: A. Administrative**

The **process or rule** requiring documents to be placed in the safe is an **administrative control**.

- Administrative controls are policies, rules, and procedures.
- The **safe itself** is a **physical control**, but the **process of using it** is administrative.
- Tangential is not a recognised control category.
- Technical controls relate to IT systems.

</details>

## Question 2

A vendor publishes different sets of instructions for installing an operating system depending on the platform used.

This is an example of a:

A. Law  
B. Procedure  
C. Standard  
D. Policy  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: B. Procedure**

A **procedure** provides step by step instructions for completing a specific task.

- Law is government issued.
- Standards are generally accepted across an industry.
- Policies are high level organisational directives.

</details>

## Question 3

Triffid Corporation publishes a policy requiring employees to act in a way that protects health and safety.  
The security office writes detailed instructions on how to wear protective gear in hazardous areas.

What are these detailed instructions?

A. Policy  
B. Procedure  
C. Standard  
D. Law  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: B. Procedure**

A **procedure** describes the exact steps to perform a task inside an organisation.

- Policy is the high level directive.
- Standards apply broadly across an industry.
- Law is issued by government.

</details>

---

## Question 4

A widely recognised set of industry security rules called the **Data Security Standard** is followed by organisations worldwide.

This is an example of a:

A. Law  
B. Policy  
C. Standard  
D. Procedure  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: C. Standard**

A **standard** is a set of rules or best practices recognised across an industry.

- Not a law because it is not issued by government.
- Not a policy because it is not internal to one organisation.
- Not a procedure because it does not describe specific steps for one task.

</details>

## Question 5

Olaf, an ISC2 member and security analyst, knows his company is not following a security standard during an audit.

What should Olaf do?

A. Tell the auditors the truth  
B. Ask supervisors for guidance  
C. Ask ISC2 for guidance  
D. Lie to the auditors  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: A. Tell the auditors the truth**

The ISC2 Code of Ethics requires members to:

- Act honestly
- Protect the profession
- Act responsibly

Therefore Olaf must report the truth.

</details>

## Question 6

Siobhan, an ISC2 member, receives a parking ticket after work.

What should she do?

A. Inform ISC2  
B. Pay the ticket  
C. Inform supervisors  
D. Resign  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: B. Pay the ticket**

A parking ticket is a minor legal issue and unrelated to professional conduct.

It does not need to be reported to ISC2 or the employer.

</details>

## Question 7

In risk management, what is **something that poses risk to an organisation or asset**?

A. Fear  
B. Threat  
C. Control  
D. Asset  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: B. Threat**

A **threat** is any potential cause of harm to an asset.

- Control mitigates risk
- Asset is something of value
- Fear is not a formal risk management term

</details>

## Question 8

A system records transactions to show which users performed certain actions.

This provides:

A. Non-repudiation  
B. Multifactor authentication  
C. Biometrics  
D. Privacy  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: A. Non-repudiation**

Non-repudiation ensures a user **cannot deny performing an action**.

Logging transactions supports this.

</details>

## Question 9

A **software firewall** blocks certain network traffic on a device.

What type of control is this?

A. Physical  
B. Administrative  
C. Passive  
D. Technical  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: D. Technical**

Technical controls are implemented using **IT systems and technology**.

Firewalls operate within the IT environment.

</details>

## Question 10

Triffid leadership publishes a document stating the company will follow **SANS best practices**.

The Triffid document is a ______ and the SANS documents are ______.

A. Law, policy  
B. Policy, standard  
C. Policy, law  
D. Procedure, procedure  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: B. Policy, Standard**

- The internal document created by management is a **policy**.
- SANS best practices are **industry standards**.

</details>

## Question 11

Phrenal sells a laptop online and hopes to get $100 but is willing to accept less.

This demonstrates:

A. Risk tolerance  
B. Risk inversion  
C. Threat  
D. Vulnerability  

<details>

Risk tolerance is the level of risk someone is willing to accept.

</details>

## Question 12

A router is configured to allow only specific MAC addresses to join the network.

This is an example of a:

A. Physical control  
B. Administrative control  
C. Substantial control  
D. Technical control  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: D. Technical**

If the control is implemented through **technology or IT systems**, it is a **technical control**.

A helpful rule of thumb often used in exams:

> If it uses electricity or is part of an IT system, it is likely a **technical control**.

</details>
