# Packet Courier Brand Spec

Reference direction: https://messenger.abeto.co/

Observed reference traits from reachable indexed sources:
- Browser-based WebGL / Three.js adventure game.
- Small planet delivery premise with playful character navigation.
- Illustrated 3D world, character configuration, NPC-style storytelling.
- Lightweight, game-like interaction rather than a conventional SaaS dashboard.

Adapted original system:
- `--bg`: `oklch(10% 0.035 245)`
- `--surface`: `oklch(18% 0.045 245)`
- `--fg`: `oklch(96% 0.012 210)`
- `--muted`: `oklch(74% 0.04 225)`
- `--border`: `oklch(39% 0.06 235)`
- `--accent`: `oklch(76% 0.18 188)`

Fonts:
- Display: `"Arial Rounded MT Bold", "Trebuchet MS", "Segoe UI", system-ui, sans-serif`
- Body: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", system-ui, sans-serif`
- Mono: `"JetBrains Mono", "IBM Plex Mono", ui-monospace, Menlo, monospace`

Layout posture:
- Build around a small cyber planet metaphor: packets, delivery routes, districts, gates.
- Use rounded chunky panels, heavy borders, and slight cartoon shadows.
- Keep the theme cyber/security through cyan route lines, telemetry labels, gates, flags, TLS, CIA, and weak-signal language.
- Avoid copying the original game assets or exact compositions; preserve the premise and interaction mood only.
- Keep study functionality and data hooks intact.
