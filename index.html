Independent study tool

Built for repetition, not certification claims.

This project is an independent study tool. It is not official ISC2 training material and is not endorsed, certified, or recognised by ISC2. Use it to practise recall, identify weak areas, and verify important topics against official ISC2 guidance.

DR

Drill Route

A delivery loop for recall. Read the prompt cold, reveal the answer, then route the packet as cleared, flagged, skipped, or ready for report.

  • Preserved study cards and domain filters
  • Weak-route queue for uncertain topics
  • Saved browser progress
  • Exportable remediation signal

QG

Quiz Gate

A checkpoint for multiple-choice decisions. Pick one route, lock the answer, inspect the rationale, and rerun missed checkpoints.

  • Preserved quiz content and explanations
  • Answer locking and visible states
  • Missed-question signal queue
  • Domain and keyword filters
