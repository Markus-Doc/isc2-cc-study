Important disclaimer

Independent study tool, not official ISC2 training

This project is an independent study tool. It is not official ISC2 training material and is not endorsed, certified, or recognised by ISC2. It was built to help students study intentionally, identify weak areas, and practise recall.

This tool does not replace official training material. Use it alongside your preferred official ISC2 resources and verify important topics against ISC2 guidance.

Recommended study loop

A simple rhythm for learning quickly without pretending weak areas are already solved.

01

Drill recall

Read a card, answer from memory, reveal the correct answer, then sort it honestly.

02

Generate report

Finish the session and use the report to see what needs more study.

03

Quiz and retest

Use quiz mode to practise exam style questions and turn missed answers into future drill topics.
